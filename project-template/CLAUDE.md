# Project rules

This folder is the working project for the dental-aftercare-video skill. Read ../SKILL.md for the full process.

1. Final video = templates/<name>/intro.mp4 + the middle video Claude makes + templates/<name>/outro.mp4.
2. Never edit, convert or overwrite anything in templates/. Only read it.
3. Never change characters/ (approved and locked) or the look in ../engine/scene.js without the owner's permission.
4. Scripts in scripts/ stay "approved": false until the owner names the dentist who approved them and the date.
   Unapproved scripts only make -DRAFT videos.
5. Never invent medical advice, medicines or doses. Pain relief is always "only what your dentist advised".
6. Free tools only, including for commercial use. 2D only.
7. Vertical video. Keep text out of the top 120 px and the bottom 320 px (of 1920).
8. No real patient names, photos or data.
9. After every render, read the report and look at the contact sheet before telling the owner it is done.
